YOUR NEXT TEN MINUTES

A new adventure.
Same old world.

Start with a fresh test world and keep a backup of any save you care about. These packs target one specific Minecraft version.

  1. 01

    Pick your pack

    Download an individual ZIP, or unpack the collection bundle first. No launcher or signup needed.

  2. 02

    Drop it into place

    With your world closed, put gameplay ZIPs in its datapacks folder. Put visual ZIPs in resourcepacks and enable them in Options.

  3. 03

    Follow the field guide

    Open a pack’s details for its start command, dependencies and removal instructions. Play on Java 1.21.1.
